# Account Deletion — in-app text

Text for the account deletion screen (SH-10). This screen is also linked
from the store listing and the privacy policy.

---

## Screen: Settings → Delete Account

### Header

> Delete your account

### Body text

> Deleting your account will permanently remove your personal data from BSAH,
> including your name, email address, and sign-in credentials.
>
> **What happens to your data:**
>
> - **Your account** is deactivated immediately and scheduled for permanent
>   deletion.
> - **Session records** (scores, notes) that involve other people (instructors,
>   cadets) may be retained in anonymised form for the academy's records, per
>   our data retention policy.
> - **Video clips** of your child's sessions will be permanently deleted.
> - **Consent records** are retained as an audit trail for a limited period,
>   as required by our legal obligations, then permanently deleted.
> - **Care logs and attendance records** (for staff accounts) may be retained
>   in anonymised form for the hub's operational records.
>
> This action cannot be undone once the deletion is completed.

### Reason field (optional)

> **Why are you leaving?** (optional, up to 1,000 characters)
>
> This helps us improve BSAH. Your reason is not shared publicly.

### Confirmation

> To confirm, type **DELETE** below:

### Confirm button

> Request account deletion

### After submission

> **Your deletion request has been submitted.**
>
> Reference: [XXXXXXXX]
>
> Our team will review your request. You will receive an email when the
> deletion is complete. You can cancel this request from Settings before
> it is processed.

### Cancel option (while pending)

> **Cancel deletion request**
>
> Your account will remain active and no data will be deleted.
