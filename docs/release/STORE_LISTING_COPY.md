## Full Description (Google Play / App Store)

> BSAH connects families with their child's horse-riding education. Parents
> and guardians can follow their cadet's progress — session scores, rank
> promotions, and achievements — all from one app.
>
> **For families:**
> - View your child's riding, ground, and stable scores after each session
> - Track rank progression and achievements
> - Watch short session clips (with your consent) recorded by the instructor
> - Manage video consent per child, and withdraw it at any time
>
> **For instructors:**
> - Check cadets in by scanning their passport QR code
> - Score riding, ground, and stable skills after each session
> - Record a short silent video clip of the session (only with parental consent)
> - Promote cadets through the rank system
>
> **For grooms:**
> - Log daily horse care with photo proof
> - Punch in at the yard with location verification
> - Track morning and evening care routines
>
> **Privacy first:**
> - Children do not have accounts — parents manage everything
> - Video recording requires explicit parental consent
> - Location is checked only when you press the button — never in the background
> - No audio is ever recorded
> - Delete your account and data at any time from Settings
>
> BSAH is used by riding schools ("hubs") to manage their programmes. Ask your
> hub if they use BSAH.
